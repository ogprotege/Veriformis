# Phase 4 Risk Register

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-21

| ID | State | Likelihood | Impact | Risk | Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P4-R1 | Mitigated | High | High | An exporter becomes a second construction, curation, or split pipeline. | The service exposes no mutation controls; fresh row-set/membership reconstruction and the consolidated omission, addition, duplication, reorder, ordinal, target, assignment, leakage-group, partition, and resplit matrix fail closed; balancing necessarily reduces to a covered membership/order change |
| P4-R2 | Mitigated | Medium | High | Self-consistency is misreported as trusted source identity. | Phase 4.3 defaults export admission to retained expected manifest SHA-256, requires explicit lower trust, and rejects evidence drift without fallback |
| P4-R3 | Mitigated | Medium | High | Verify-then-read races allow source substitution. | Phase 4.4 derives every plan source fact and membership baseline from the immutable row and provenance semantics captured during descriptor-anchored verification; it does not reopen source paths |
| P4-R4 | Mitigated | High | High | Traversal, links, aliases, or races escape the destination. | Portable paths, descriptor-anchored staging/walks, closed regular-file trees, Unicode/case-alias checks, link/special-file refusal, target-race preservation, and atomic no-replace promotion pass the closeout harness |
| P4-R5 | Mitigated | Medium | High | A failure leaves a partial or falsely rolled-back export. | In-staging receipt verification, one promotion, owned-object cleanup, destination-race preservation, and explicit verifiable visible-partial outcomes pass the closeout harness |
| P4-R6 | Mitigated | Medium | Medium | Cancellation after visibility is reported as rollback. | Final pre-visibility cancellation leaves no artifact; a simultaneous post-promotion signal preserves and verifies the successful publication instead of reporting rollback |
| P4-R7 | Mitigated | Medium | Medium | Dependency-specific bytes are called portable deterministic bytes. | Two independent renders compare complete exact-byte trees or versioned canonical semantic preimages and membership while exact renderer/replayer dependencies remain bound |
| P4-R8 | Mitigated | Medium | High | CLI, MCP, or Mac code reimplements export policy. | Phase 4.8 surfaces delegate to `PipelineService`, share one canonical protocol and frozen evidence fixture, and the Mac bridge shells the CLI without destination logic |
| P4-R9 | Mitigated | High | High | The conformance exporter is mistaken for a supported Phase 5 container. | The implementation remains test-injected, production discovery is empty, and taxonomy/support capability lists promote no generic container or new profile |
| P4-R10 | Mitigated | Medium | High | Phase 4 absorbs generic containers or trainer profiles. | Phase 4 closes without a shipped implementation; every Phase 5/8/9/10 container/profile remains planned or candidate until its own packet and evidence gate |
| P4-R11 | Mitigated | Medium | High | A semantic replayer copies the planned digest or reports decoded rows unrelated to produced bytes. | Private replay returns canonical preimage bytes and normalized membership; the service computes digests, reuses the complete membership gate, and replays descriptor-read staging |
| P4-R12 | Mitigated | Medium | Medium | A v1 verification object is mistaken for durable proof that two renders occurred. | The contract and frozen schemas state that rerender comparison is runtime admission evidence; v1 contains no transcript and durable attestation requires a new version |
| P4-R13 | Mitigated | Medium | High | Host or dependency defaults make semantic canonicalization ambiguous or unbounded. | The only implementation is a statically bounded private fixture; any future supported semantic profile must bind exact dependencies and enforce Unicode/order/error plus byte, record, nesting, and applicable resource limits before admission |
| P4-R14 | Mitigated | Medium | High | Adapter cancellation or mixed stdout/stderr falsely reports a published destination as rolled back or decodes diagnostics as authority. | CLI/MCP cooperative cancellation, complete-worker draining, separate canonical stdout, diagnostic stderr, bounded streams, response-less forced-termination ambiguity, and visibility-race tests pass |
